A letter for Aaru

Aaru,

I know sirf “sorry” bol dene se woh sab undo nahi ho jaata jo maine kiya.

Maine tumhe beech mein chhod diya tha. Maine breakup ka decision liya, aur shayad us waqt mujhe laga tha ki main jo kar raha hoon woh sahi hai.

But now, looking back, I realise ki maine galti ki thi.

Tumhe hurt karna, tumse door chale jaana... ye sab cheezein ab samajh aati hain ki kitni wrong thi.

Main ye nahi bolunga ki “mujhe ek chance do kyunki main tumhare bina nahi reh sakta.”

Main bas itna bolunga ki I want to become better.

Better at understanding you. Better at communicating. Aur sabse important — better at valuing what I have before I lose it.

Mujhe nahi pata tumhare dil mein mere liye ab kya hai. Shayad gussa ho. Hurt ho. Confused ho.

Aur honestly, tum jo feel karti ho, woh valid hai.

But agar mere liye tumhare dil mein thodi si bhi jagah abhi baaki hai...

Can we try again?

Not to repeat the old story.

But to write a better one this time. ❤️

Aaru...

I can't change what happened.

But I can choose what happens next.
